\section{Methodology}

The 16- and 60-feature observation vectors inherited from the AuctionNet
baseline are point-statistic snapshots: per-tick means, last-three-tick
views, and episode-historical means of bid, cost, and win-rate signals. They
expose neither the \emph{time derivatives} of these signals (whether
competition is heating up or cooling down), the \emph{distribution shape} of
competitor bids (volatility, right-tail ``whales''), nor any explicit
\emph{opponent-aggression proxy}. Because AuctionNet is a partially
observable MDP and the 47 fixed competitors' bids are never directly
observable, every non-Markov signal the policy might need must be compressed
into the obs vector. We therefore augment the observation rather than the
policy class or the reward.

Let $o_t^{(60)} \in \mathbb{R}^{60}$ denote the baseline observation. We
define the market-aware observation
\begin{equation}
  o_t \;=\;
  \big[\,o_t^{(60)};\;\phi^{(1)}_t;\;\phi^{(2)}_t\,\big]
  \;\in\;\mathbb{R}^{98},
\end{equation}
where $\phi^{(1)}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{20}$ (Tier~1) selects statistics
\texttt{BiddingEnv} already computes per tick but does not surface to the
policy, and $\phi^{(2)}_t \in \mathbb{R}^{18}$ (Tier~2) is derived from the
existing per-tick history cache via a windowed least-squares slope
$\mathrm{slope}_W$, a windowed standard deviation $\mathrm{std}_W$ over the
most recent $W{=}5$ ticks, and the episode-history $z$-score
$z_t(x)=(x_t-\bar x_{1:t})/\mathrm{std}_{1:t}(x)$. The 38 added features
group into six interpretable categories (competitor distribution shape,
opponent-aggression proxies, slot dynamics, regime $z$-scores, momentum, and
a budget-pacing residual); their definitions are listed in the Appendix.

The simulator (\texttt{step}, \texttt{reset}, \texttt{\_place\_bids},
\texttt{\_update\_history}), action interface, and reward function are all
left unchanged; the extension is confined to \texttt{\_get\_state\_dict} and
a new JSON observation selector. It targets the observation \emph{layer} of
the pipeline and is therefore orthogonal to the reward-shaping,
sample-reweighting, temporal-encoding, and LLM-policy variants. It is
trained with the same PPO objective, hyperparameters, and 10M-step budget as
the obs-60 baseline; the only difference is the input dimension of the
actor and critic MLPs.

\section{Appendix}

\subsection*{Windowed primitives}

For any tracked signal $x_{1:t}$ and window $W{=}5$,
\begin{equation}
  \mathrm{slope}_W(x_{t}) \;=\;
  \frac{\sum_{k=0}^{W-1}\!\big(k-\bar k\big)\big(x_{t-W+1+k}-\bar x\big)}
       {\sum_{k=0}^{W-1}\!\big(k-\bar k\big)^{2}},
  \qquad
  \mathrm{std}_W(x_{t}) \;=\;
  \sqrt{\tfrac{1}{W}\!\sum_{k=0}^{W-1}\!\big(x_{t-W+1+k}-\bar x\big)^{2}}.
\end{equation}

\subsection*{Tier 2 feature categories}

Below $\mathrm{lwc}_t$ denotes the within-tick mean of the least-winning
competitor cost (the bid floor), $b_t$ our own bid mean, $p_t$ the mean
predicted conversion probability, $\mathrm{wr}_t$ the win rate, and
$C_t,B,T_{\!\text{left}}$ the cumulative cost, total budget, and remaining
time fraction.
\begin{itemize}\itemsep 2pt
  \item \textbf{Competitor distribution shape} (7 keys): trend
        $\mathrm{slope}_5(\mathrm{lwc}_t)$, dispersion
        $\mathrm{std}_5(\mathrm{lwc}_t)$, coefficient of variation
        $\mathrm{std}_5/\mathrm{mean}_5$, the current-tick $z$-score
        $z_t(\mathrm{lwc})$, and the right-tail width
        $\mathrm{lwc}^{(01\%)}_t-\mathrm{lwc}^{(10\%)}_t$ that exposes
        ``whale'' bidders the mean alone smooths out.
  \item \textbf{Opponent-aggression proxies} (2 keys): since opponent bids
        are unobserved, we infer them from auction outcomes via
        $\alpha^{\mathrm{opp}}_t = \mathrm{lwc}_t/p_t$ and our own
        competitiveness $\beta_t = b_t/\mathrm{lwc}_t$.
  \item \textbf{Slot dynamics} (3 keys): the cost ratio $c^{(1)}_t/c^{(3)}_t$
        between top and floor ad slot, the analogous exposure ratio, and the
        slope of slot concentration over the last five ticks.
  \item \textbf{Regime $z$-scores} (2 keys): $z_t$ of pv-volume intensity and
        of win rate, locating the current tick within the episode's own
        history rather than against an absolute scale.
  \item \textbf{Momentum / acceleration} (3 keys): $\mathrm{slope}_5$ of
        $\mathrm{wr}_t$, of the ROI proxy $p_t/\mathrm{lwc}_t$, and of the
        per-tick conversion rate.
  \item \textbf{Pacing residual} (1 key):
        $\rho_t = C_t/B - (1-T_{\!\text{left}})$, with $\rho_t>0$ indicating
        overpacing and $\rho_t<0$ underpacing.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Tier 1 selections (20 keys)}

Per-slot win counts, per-slot exposure rates, raw conversion volume,
last-/last-three-/historical CPA exceedence rate, and episode cumulatives
($Y_t,C_t,\mathrm{CPA}_t$). These are already maintained by
\texttt{\_update\_history}; only the observation selector changes.
